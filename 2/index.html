Topik 1: Lanjutan Turunan Parsial (10 Soal)

  1. Diberikan $f(x, y) = x^4 y^3 - 3x^2 y^5$. Tentukan $f_{xx}$.
  2. Diberikan $f(x, y) = \sin(x^2 y)$. Tentukan $f_{yy}$.
  3. Diberikan $f(x, y) = e^{2x+3y}$. Tentukan $f_{xy}$.
  4. Diberikan $f(x, y) = \ln(x^2 + y^2)$. Tentukan $f_{yx}$.
  5. Verifikasi Teorema Clairaut ($f_{xy} = f_{yx}$) untuk $f(x, y) = x^3 y^4$.
  6. Tentukan semua turunan parsial orde kedua ($f_{xx}, f_{yy}, f_{xy}, f_{yx}$) untuk $f(x, y) = x \cos(y)$.
  7. Jika $z = \frac{x}{y}$, tentukan $\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2}$.
  8. Jika $z = \frac{x}{y}$, tentukan $\frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2}$.
  9. Diberikan $f(x, y, z) = x y^2 z^3$. Tentukan $f_{xyz}$.
  10. Tentukan $f_{xxy}$ jika $f(x, y) = e^{xy^2}$.

Topik 2: Diferensial Fungsi dari fungsi (Aturan Rantai) (10 Soal)

  1. Tuliskan rumus Aturan Rantai untuk $\frac{dz}{dt}$ jika $z = f(x, y)$, $x = g(t)$, dan $y = h(t)$.
  2. Gunakan Aturan Rantai untuk mencari $\frac{dz}{dt}$ jika $z = x^2 y$, $x = t^2$, dan $y = t^3$.
  3. Gunakan Aturan Rantai untuk mencari $\frac{dz}{dt}$ jika $z = \ln(x+y)$, $x = e^t$, dan $y = e^{-t}$.
  4. Tuliskan rumus Aturan Rantai untuk $\frac{\partial z}{\partial s}$ jika $z = f(x, y)$, $x = g(s, t)$, dan $y = h(s, t)$.
  5. Gunakan Aturan Rantai untuk mencari $\frac{\partial z}{\partial s}$ jika $z = x^2 - y^2$, $x = s \cos(t)$, dan $y = s \sin(t)$.
  6. Untuk soal nomor 5, carilah $\frac{\partial z}{\partial t}$.
  7. Cari $\frac{dw}{dt}$ pada $t=1$ jika $w = x y z$, $x = t$, $y = t^2$, $z = t^3$.
  8. Cari $\frac{\partial w}{\partial u}$ jika $w = x e^y$, $x = u+v$, $y = u-v$.
  9. Cari $\frac{\partial w}{\partial v}$ jika $w = x e^y$, $x = u+v$, $y = u-v$.
  10. Jika $V = \pi r^2 h$ dan radius $r$ bertambah pada laju $2$ cm/s sementara tinggi $h$ berkurang pada laju $1$ cm/s, tentukan $\frac{dV}{dt}$ pada saat $r=5$ cm dan $h=10$ cm.

Topik 3: Fungsi Implisit (10 Soal)

  1. Jika $F(x, y) = 0$, tuliskan rumus untuk $\frac{dy}{dx}$ menggunakan turunan parsial.
  2. Gunakan turunan implisit untuk mencari $\frac{dy}{dx}$ jika $x^2 + y^2 = 36$.
  3. Gunakan turunan implisit untuk mencari $\frac{dy}{dx}$ jika $x^3 + y^3 = 6xy$.
  4. Gunakan turunan implisit untuk mencari $\frac{dy}{dx}$ jika $\sin(x+y) = y^2 \cos(x)$.
  5. Jika $F(x, y, z) = 0$, tuliskan rumus untuk $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$.
  6. Cari $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$ jika $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$.
  7. Cari $\frac{\partial z}{\partial y}$ jika $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 1$.
  8. Cari $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$ jika $x y^2 + y z^2 + z x^2 = 0$.
  9. Cari $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x}$ pada $(1, 1, 1)$ jika $x y z = 1$.
  10. Cari $\frac{dy}{dx}$ pada $(1, 1)$ jika $x e^y + y e^x = 2e$.

Topik 4: Jacobian (10 Soal)

  1. Definisikan determinan Jacobian $J$ untuk transformasi $x = x(u, v)$, $y = y(u, v)$.
  2. Hitung Jacobian $J = \frac{\partial(x, y)}{\partial(u, v)}$ untuk $x = u+v$, $y = u-v$.
  3. Hitung Jacobian $J = \frac{\partial(x, y)}{\partial(r, \theta)}$ untuk transformasi koordinat polar $x = r \cos(\theta)$, $y = r \sin(\theta)$.
  4. Hitung Jacobian $J = \frac{\partial(x, y)}{\partial(u, v)}$ untuk $x = 2u + 3v$, $y = 4u - v$.
  5. Hitung Jacobian $J = \frac{\partial(x, y)}{\partial(u, v)}$ untuk $x = u^2 - v^2$, $y = 2uv$.
  6. Apa signifikansi determinan Jacobian dalam integral lipat dua?
  7. Definisikan matriks Jacobian untuk $F: \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^2$ di mana $F(x, y) = (f(x, y), g(x, y))$.
  8. Tentukan matriks Jacobian untuk $F(x, y) = (x^2 y, x \sin(y))$.
  9. Definisikan Jacobian $J = \frac{\partial(x, y, z)}{\partial(\rho, \phi, \theta)}$ untuk koordinat bola (tulis bentuk determinannya).
  10. Hitung Jacobian $J = \frac{\partial(x, y, z)}{\partial(u, v, w)}$ untuk $x = u$, $y = u+v$, $z = u+v+w$.

Pembahasan & Kunci Jawaban

Topik 1: Lanjutan Turunan Parsial

  1. $f_x = 4x^3 y^3 - 6x y^5 \implies f_{xx} = 12x^2 y^3 - 6y^5$
  2. $f_y = \cos(x^2 y) \cdot x^2 \implies f_{yy} = -\sin(x^2 y) \cdot x^2 \cdot x^2 = -x^4 \sin(x^2 y)$
  3. $f_x = e^{2x+3y} \cdot 2 \implies f_{xy} = (e^{2x+3y} \cdot 2) \cdot 3 = 6e^{2x+3y}$
  4. $f_y = \frac{1}{x^2+y^2} \cdot 2y = \frac{2y}{x^2+y^2} \implies f_{yx} = \frac{\partial}{\partial x} (\frac{2y}{x^2+y^2}) = -2y(x^2+y^2)^{-2} \cdot 2x = \frac{-4xy}{(x^2+y^2)^2}$
  5. $f_x = 3x^2 y^4 \implies f_{xy} = 12x^2 y^3$. $f_y = 4x^3 y^3 \implies f_{yx} = 12x^2 y^3$. Terverifikasi.
  6. $f_x = \cos(y)$, $f_y = -x \sin(y)$. Maka: $f_{xx} = 0$, $f_{yy} = -x \cos(y)$, $f_{xy} = -\sin(y)$, $f_{yx} = -\sin(y)$.
  7. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = \frac{1}{y} \implies \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial x^2} = 0$
  8. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = -x y^{-2} = -\frac{x}{y^2} \implies \frac{\partial^2 z}{\partial y^2} = 2x y^{-3} = \frac{2x}{y^3}$
  9. $f_x = y^2 z^3 \implies f_{xy} = 2y z^3 \implies f_{xyz} = 6y z^2$
  10. $f_x = e^{xy^2} \cdot y^2 \implies f_{xx} = (e^{xy^2} \cdot y^2) \cdot y^2 = y^4 e^{xy^2} \implies f_{xxy} = 4y^3 e^{xy^2} + y^4 (e^{xy^2} \cdot 2xy) = (4y^3 + 2xy^5)e^{xy^2}$

Topik 2: Diferensial Fungsi dari fungsi (Aturan Rantai)

  1. $\frac{dz}{dt} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \frac{dx}{dt} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \frac{dy}{dt}$
  2. $\frac{dz}{dt} = (2xy)(2t) + (x^2)(3t^2) = 2(t^2)(t^3)(2t) + (t^2)^2(3t^2) = 4t^6 + 3t^6 = 7t^6$
  3. $\frac{dz}{dt} = (\frac{1}{x+y})(e^t) + (\frac{1}{x+y})(-e^{-t}) = \frac{e^t - e^{-t}}{e^t + e^{-t}}$
  4. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial s} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x} \frac{\partial x}{\partial s} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y} \frac{\partial y}{\partial s}$
  5. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial s} = (2x)(\cos(t)) + (-2y)(\sin(t)) = 2(s \cos t)(\cos t) - 2(s \sin t)(\sin t) = 2s(\cos^2 t - \sin^2 t) = 2s \cos(2t)$
  6. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial t} = (2x)(-s \sin t) + (-2y)(s \cos t) = 2(s \cos t)(-s \sin t) - 2(s \sin t)(s \cos t) = -2s^2 \sin t \cos t - 2s^2 \sin t \cos t = -4s^2 \sin t \cos t = -2s^2 \sin(2t)$
  7. $w = t \cdot t^2 \cdot t^3 = t^6 \implies \frac{dw}{dt} = 6t^5$. Pada $t=1$, $\frac{dw}{dt} = 6$.
  8. $\frac{\partial w}{\partial u} = \frac{\partial w}{\partial x} \frac{\partial x}{\partial u} + \frac{\partial w}{\partial y} \frac{\partial y}{\partial u} = (e^y)(1) + (x e^y)(1) = e^y (1+x) = e^{u-v}(1 + u+v)$
  9. $\frac{\partial w}{\partial v} = \frac{\partial w}{\partial x} \frac{\partial x}{\partial v} + \frac{\partial w}{\partial y} \frac{\partial y}{\partial v} = (e^y)(1) + (x e^y)(-1) = e^y (1-x) = e^{u-v}(1 - (u+v))$
  10. $\frac{dV}{dt} = \frac{\partial V}{\partial r} \frac{dr}{dt} + \frac{\partial V}{\partial h} \frac{dh}{dt} = (2\pi rh)\frac{dr}{dt} + (\pi r^2)\frac{dh}{dt} = 2\pi(5)(10)(2) + \pi(5)^2(-1) = 200\pi - 25\pi = 175\pi$ cm$^3$/s

Topik 3: Fungsi Implisit

  1. $\frac{dy}{dx} = -\frac{F_x}{F_y} = -\frac{\partial F / \partial x}{\partial F / \partial y}$
  2. $2x + 2y \frac{dy}{dx} = 0 \implies \frac{dy}{dx} = -\frac{2x}{2y} = -\frac{x}{y}$
  3. $3x^2 + 3y^2 \frac{dy}{dx} = 6y + 6x \frac{dy}{dx} \implies (3y^2 - 6x)\frac{dy}{dx} = 6y - 3x^2 \implies \frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{6y - 3x^2}{3y^2 - 6x} = \frac{2y - x^2}{y^2 - 2x}$
  4. $\cos(x+y) (1 + \frac{dy}{dx}) = 2y \frac{dy}{dx} \cos(x) + y^2 (-\sin(x)) \implies \frac{dy}{dx} (\cos(x+y) - 2y \cos(x)) = -y^2 \sin(x) - \cos(x+y) \implies \frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{y^2 \sin(x) + \cos(x+y)}{2y \cos(x) - \cos(x+y)}$
  5. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{F_x}{F_z} = -\frac{\partial F / \partial x}{\partial F / \partial z}$
  6. $F(x,y,z) = x^2+y^2+z^2-1=0$. $F_x = 2x$, $F_z = 2z$. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{2x}{2z} = -\frac{x}{z}$
  7. $F(x,y,z) = x^2+y^2+z^2-1=0$. $F_y = 2y$, $F_z = 2z$. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial y} = -\frac{2y}{2z} = -\frac{y}{z}$
  8. $F(x,y,z) = x y^2 + y z^2 + z x^2 = 0$. $F_x = y^2 + 2xz$, $F_z = 2yz + x^2$. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{y^2 + 2xz}{2yz + x^2}$
  9. $F(x,y,z) = xyz - 1 = 0$. $F_x = yz$, $F_z = xy$. $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -\frac{yz}{xy} = -\frac{z}{x}$. Pada $(1,1,1)$, $\frac{\partial z}{\partial x} = -1$.
  10. $(1 \cdot e^y + x e^y \frac{dy}{dx}) + (\frac{dy}{dx} e^x + y e^x) = 0$. Pada (1,1): $e + e \frac{dy}{dx} + e \frac{dy}{dx} + e = 0 \implies 2e \frac{dy}{dx} = -2e \implies \frac{dy}{dx} = -1$.

Topik 4: Jacobian

  1. $J = \frac{\partial(x, y)}{\partial(u, v)} = \det \begin{vmatrix} \frac{\partial x}{\partial u} & \frac{\partial x}{\partial v} \\ \frac{\partial y}{\partial u} & \frac{\partial y}{\partial v} \end{vmatrix} = \frac{\partial x}{\partial u} \frac{\partial y}{\partial v} - \frac{\partial x}{\partial v} \frac{\partial y}{\partial u}$
  2. $J = \det \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = (1)(-1) - (1)(1) = -2$
  3. $J = \det \begin{vmatrix} \cos(\theta) & -r \sin(\theta) \\ \sin(\theta) & r \cos(\theta) \end{vmatrix} = r \cos^2(\theta) - (-r \sin^2(\theta)) = r(\cos^2 \theta + \sin^2 \theta) = r$
  4. $J = \det \begin{vmatrix} 2 & 3 \\ 4 & -1 \end{vmatrix} = (2)(-1) - (3)(4) = -2 - 12 = -14$
  5. $J = \det \begin{vmatrix} 2u & -2v \\ 2v & 2u \end{vmatrix} = (2u)(2u) - (-2v)(2v) = 4u^2 + 4v^2 = 4(u^2+v^2)$
  6. Sebagai faktor skala diferensial area, $dA_{xy} = |J| dA_{uv}$. Digunakan untuk mengubah variabel dalam integral lipat.
  7. $J_F = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial f}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial f}{\partial y} \\ \frac{\partial g}{\partial x} & \frac{\partial g}{\partial y} \end{bmatrix}$
  8. $f(x,y) = x^2 y$, $g(x,y) = x \sin(y)$. $J_F = \begin{bmatrix} 2xy & x^2 \\ \sin(y) & x \cos(y) \end{bmatrix}$
  9. $J = \det \begin{vmatrix} \frac{\partial x}{\partial \rho} & \frac{\partial x}{\partial \phi} & \frac{\partial x}{\partial \theta} \\ \frac{\partial y}{\partial \rho} & \frac{\partial y}{\partial \phi} & \frac{\partial y}{\partial \theta} \\ \frac{\partial z}{\partial \rho} & \frac{\partial z}{\partial \phi} & \frac{\partial z}{\partial \theta} \end{vmatrix}$ (Hasilnya adalah $\rho^2 \sin(\phi)$)
  10. $J = \det \begin{vmatrix} 1 & 0 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 0 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 \end{vmatrix} = 1((1)(1) - (0)(1)) - 0(...) + 0(...) = 1$
